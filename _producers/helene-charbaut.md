Hélène Charbaut is the fourth generation of the family behind Champagne Guy Charbaut in Mareuil-sur-Aÿ, in the Vallée de la Marne. After several years of experience in Burgundy, she returned to the family estate in 2020. There she started making Champagne under her own name, with what she had learned in Burgundy.

Hélène wants to do as little as possible in the cellar, so that the terroir can speak. She vinifies each plot separately, in barrel, mainly using second-hand barrels from Burgundy. She only uses the cuvée, the first and best part of the pressing. The wines are not filtered. She’s sparing with sulphur, but always wants her wines to be clean. The plan is for her to gradually take over part of her father’s vineyards.
