Felix Walter grew up in Bürgstadt, in Franconia, on the family estate Weingut Josef Walter. His father Christoph has been making Burgundy-inspired Spätburgunder there for years. Felix learned the craft at Künstler in the Rheingau and with Julian Huber in Baden, among others. There he fell under the spell of Chardonnay and Pinot Noir from Burgundy, and the idea arose to make wines of his own that are different from those of the family estate. The name Krawalter is a nickname from his training, which he got because he always asked critical questions.

After that he worked abroad for a while. Among other places, he was at Hans Herzog in New Zealand, the Grabenwerkstatt in the Wachau, Mac Forbes in Australia, Bodega Chacra in Patagonia, Chanterêves in Burgundy and Laherte Frères in Champagne. In 2022 he made his first wine of his own at his parents' estate. The grapes come from the Erste Lagen in Bürgstadt. The wines ferment spontaneously and are not filtered. He makes Chardonnay, Spätburgunder and a Blanc de Noirs without dosage.

We found our way to Felix through Chanterêves, who posted a glowing review of his wines on Instagram. When we tasted the wines, we were immediately enthusiastic. They are certainly Burgundian in style, but also have a character all of their own. You can clearly taste Huber's influence: the white wines have a nice edge of reduction.
