Morgane and Alban Desazars have been making wine in Auxey-Duresses, in the Côte d'Or, since 2023. Morgane grew up in Champagne. She is the daughter of Laurent Champs of Champagne Vilmart and later worked at Olivier Leflaive. Alban comes from the Loire and worked as a consultant for other growers. In Auxey-Duresses they bought an old wine estate with cellars, some of which are carved directly into the limestone. They make small quantities of wine plot by plot, largely from the higher-lying Hautes-Côtes.

What struck us at Ex Nihilo is how much attention Alban pays to pressing. In Champagne people talk about it endlessly, in Burgundy much less so. Alban starts at a pressure of only 0.25 bar and lets the juice run slowly from the press for two to three hours. Along the way he keeps tasting, and he stops as soon as the juice is at its best.

The wines ferment spontaneously, and in the cellar everything moves by gravity. Morgane and Alban do as little as possible in the cellar. The result is fresh, mineral wines without much oak, with a fine reduction.
