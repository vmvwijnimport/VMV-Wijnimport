Jonas Collino makes Champagne in Avize, one of the grand cru villages of the Côte des Blancs. The estate goes back to the 1960s, when the Petit family had its first vineyards there. Jonas is now in charge. He works with old Chardonnay vines, in a natural way, and since 2025 the estate has been certified organic. Jonas's first releases were made with bought-in grapes, to finance his project and to be able to buy barrels. His first release from his own grapes has just come out (vintage 2020).

Jonas makes Champagnes by plot, so you can really taste the different plots of Avize. His first vintage is 2020, with Le Haut du Moulin, from a plot on the south side of the village. He picks the grapes at high ripeness and uses little sulphite, only at pressing for protection. The wine then ages for ten months on the lees in barrel. In 2020 he chose a dosage of 3 grams.

We first came across Jonas during the Printemps des Champagnes. Funnily enough, that was not at one of the tastings but out on the street, at a party at Sacré Burger. In the middle of all the commotion we got hold of a glass of his Champagne and knew straight away that we had to visit him. After that visit we had no doubts left: Jonas has a big future in Champagne. On top of that, he is one of the funniest and most cheerful winemakers we know.
