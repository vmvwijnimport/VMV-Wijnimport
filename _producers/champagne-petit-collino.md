Jonas Collino makes Champagne in Avize, one of the grand cru villages of the Côte des Blancs. The estate dates back to the 1960s, when the Petit family planted its first vineyards there. Today, Jonas runs it. He works with old Chardonnay vines, in a natural way, and since 2025 the estate has been certified organic. Jonas’s first releases were made with bought-in grapes, to fund the project and buy barrels. His first release from his own grapes has just come out (vintage 2020).

Jonas makes single-plot Champagnes, so you can really taste the differences between the plots of Avize. His first vintage is 2020, with Le Haut du Moulin, from a plot on the south side of the village. He picks the grapes at high ripeness and uses very little sulphur, only at pressing to protect the juice. The wine then ages for ten months on the lees in barrel. In 2020 he chose a dosage of 3 grams.

We first came across Jonas during the Printemps des Champagnes. Funnily enough, it wasn’t at one of the tastings but out on the street, at a party at Sacré Burger. Amid all the bustle, someone handed us a glass of his Champagne and we knew straight away that we had to visit him. After that visit we had no doubts: Jonas has a great future in Champagne. He’s also one of the funniest, most cheerful winemakers we know.
