With Rosanna Sandri we have our first producer from outside France in our portfolio. The family makes wine in Treiso, in Piedmont, close to Barbaresco. It started in 1950, when Rosanna's parents Mario and Teresina planted their first vines in San Rocco Seno d'Elvio. Rosanna grew up among the vines and worked in the vineyard all her life. Today her sons Roberto and Mauro Prandi run the estate. Both studied at the wine school in Alba.

The family farms about 4 hectares, in San Rocco Seno d'Elvio and in the Rizzi vineyard in Treiso. They never use herbicides and leave the grass between the rows, which is good for the soil and for biodiversity. Pruning and picking are done by hand. In the cellar Roberto works as calmly as possible. The wines ferment with wild yeasts and only get a little sulphite when needed.

The flagship is the Barbaresco Rizzi, made from Nebbiolo from the Rizzi vineyard in Treiso. The Barbaresco Damasco comes from a small vineyard in Treiso with sandier limestone soil. The Langhe Nebbiolo Botero is the more approachable Nebbiolo. It ferments in small concrete vats, macerates on the skins for around forty days and then ages for six months in large wooden casks. The Dolcetto d'Alba Grasmeri comes from vines more than 55 years old, on sandy limestone soil with marl and sandstone. They also make a Barbera d'Alba and a white wine, the Langhe Arneis Stella Dorata.

The wines are elegant and fine, but clearly have the character of Nebbiolo and the other grapes of the region. You can really taste where they come from. The wines can certainly age, but are already very approachable when young.
