Yaël and Jonathan Sacy are sister and brother, and the thirteenth generation of a family of growers in Verzy, a grand cru village on the Montagne de Reims. They took over the family estate in 2018. Under the name Sacy Sœur & Frère, they bring their own take on the family’s terroirs. The estate used to make mostly blends; now they make Champagnes from a single village or even a single plot.

They currently make six cuvées, including a Pinot Noir from the lieu-dit Les Courtisols, a Chardonnay from two plots in Verzy, a rosé de macération from Les Bussets and Les Buissons, and a Chardonnay from Cernay-lès-Reims. They also make a Coteaux Champenois every year, a still wine from Verzy. Whether it’s red or white depends on the year. In some years it’s a white wine made from Pinot Noir, a blanc de noirs, which you don’t see often.

Yaël and Jonathan work organically, because in their view living soils make living wines. In the cellar, they’re always working on new projects and experiments. Their Champagnes are vinous and go very well with food.

Yaël and Jonathan were the first winemakers we started working with. That is why their wines have a special place in our portfolio.
