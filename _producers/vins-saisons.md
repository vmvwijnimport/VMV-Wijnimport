Vins Saisons is a small négoce in Burgundy, based in the old cellar of Rougeot in Meloisey. Loïc Lamy and Éric Pignal started it together in 2019. Both had learned the trade at estates such as Roulot, Roumier, Dujac and de Montille. In 2021 Éric left to become chef de cave at Simon Bize. Since then, Loïc has run it on his own.

Loïc works as naturally as possible, starting with buying the grapes. He only buys grapes from growers who work organically or biodynamically. In the cellar, too, he does as little as possible. He always works with 100 percent whole bunches. He does few punch-downs, and when he does, very gently. Pump-overs are rare too, and at his place they’re really bucket-overs, since he doesn’t use a pump. He adds as little sulphur as possible.

What surprised us most in the cellar were the barrels. Loïc uses 350-litre barrels instead of the usual 228 litres, because they give him more airiness and florality. The fruit steps back a little and more floral and herbal notes come through. We tasted two barrels side by side and the difference was striking.

Loïc loves wines that are light and easy to drink, and that’s how he makes them. The red wines are fruity, but certainly not simple. The stems, the larger barrels and the gentle extraction give them a lot of depth.
