Thomas Perseval is the third generation of a family of growers in Chamery, a premier cru village on the Montagne de Reims. He came back to the family estate in the early 2000s. Before that he had worked with biodynamic winemakers in Burgundy, and that strongly shaped his way of working. In 2009 he started converting the vineyards to organic, and he has been certified since 2012.

Thomas farms 2.5 hectares, spread over six plots. Each plot gets the attention it needs. In the cellar the wines ferment spontaneously and age in oak barrels. He only adds sulphite when needed. The soils in Chamery vary quite a bit, with clay, sand and chalk, and you can taste that. He makes Champagnes from individual plots, each with a character of its own. He also makes Art'terre, a blend of his vineyards that shows the estate as a whole.

We knew Thomas's Champagnes before we started importing ourselves. Whenever we were in Champagne, we regularly went looking for his bottles. When we heard that Thomas was not yet imported into the Netherlands, we did not have to think for long. We are proud to be working with his wines now.
