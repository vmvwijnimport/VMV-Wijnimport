Together, Alexis and Jeanne Limar farm 3 hectares in Champagne, with Pinot Noir, Meunier and Chardonnay. Their vineyards lie in La Neuville-aux-Larris, Cuchery and Belval-sous-Châtillon in the Vallée de la Marne, and in the grand cru village of Mailly-Champagne on the Montagne de Reims. Alexis studied viticulture and oenology at the Viti Campus in Avize and learned the craft further at his grandfather's estate. That is where he made his first two Champagnes, from the 2018 harvest. When his grandfather retired in 2023, Alexis and Jeanne took over the vineyards completely.

His grandfather gave Alexis the freedom to do things his own way early on. He stopped using pesticides and herbicides altogether. In the cellar the wines ferment spontaneously. They are neither fined nor filtered. At first Alexis and Jeanne only used stainless steel tanks, but since 2023 they ferment and age everything in small Burgundian barrels.

The dosage is low, so that freshness and minerality come first. The fruit is restrained, with fine yeasty notes in the background. These are slender, balanced Champagnes with a lot of finesse.
