Pierre-Gabriel Diarra grew up in Hautvillers, where his family owned vineyards. He first chose something completely different and worked in the tech sector in England and France for years, but in the end he went back to the vineyard. He earned his degree in viticulture and oenology in Bordeaux and made his first vintage under the name Champagne INSU in 2021.

He presses his grapes at Aurélien Lurquin's, his friend and mentor and one of the most sought-after names in Champagne. Like Lurquin, he makes wine first and Champagne second. Pierre-Gabriel farms six plots in Hautvillers, all organically, and looks at his vineyard more like a Burgundian winemaker than a Champagne maker. Every cuvée comes from one plot, one grape variety and one year. The wines ferment spontaneously, age in barrel and are neither filtered nor fined.

He gives his wines five years, with several years in barrel and a long ageing in bottle. Production is small and he is only at the beginning, but as far as we are concerned Pierre-Gabriel already belongs among the talents of the new generation in Champagne.
