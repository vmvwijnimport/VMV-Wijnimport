Pierric Brochet comes from a family of growers in Taissy, just south of Reims. He named his estate after his great-grandfather Jules, because he was too modest to put his own name on the label. He studied at the Viti Campus in Avize and did an internship with Anselme Selosse. After that he went back to the family estate. He soon realised he had more in common with Anselme’s way of working than with his father’s, and he decided to go his own way.

Talk to Pierric and you immediately understand what matters to him: terroir. He intervenes as little as possible, because he wants to let the wines speak for themselves. He spends a lot of time in the vineyard and gives each plot exactly what it needs. You can taste that precision in the wines.

After our first visit we walked away with the kind of smile you only get from very special wines. Since then, the quality has amazed us on every visit. In our view, Pierric is one of the great talents of the new generation, and there’s plenty more to come.

Demand for Pierric's wines is very high. That is why we mainly offer them to restaurants and bars in the Netherlands, so that as many people as possible can taste them.
