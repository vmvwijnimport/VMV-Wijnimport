Through our work with Pierric Brochet we had already heard about Théo Cecchetti and his Champagne, but we first tasted it during Champagne Day in Reims, at Le Wine Bar by Le Vintage. The explosive nose struck us immediately. It was the kind of wine you keep thinking about for a long time.

Théo's project is very small. His Champagne comes from one plot in Barzy-sur-Marne, Les Mocque-Souris. The vineyard was planted in 1983 and faces south-west. Pierric Brochet made the Pinot Noir from this plot available to Théo, so that he could make his own Champagne from it.

Les Mocque-Souris is one hundred percent Pinot Noir. The wine ages in oak barrels and then spends 36 months on the lees. Théo does as little as possible with the wine. The current release is the 2022.

Because there are only very few bottles, we only offer this Champagne to restaurants and bars in the Netherlands. That way as many people as possible can taste it.
