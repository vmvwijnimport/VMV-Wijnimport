Julien Hénin comes from a winemaking family in Aÿ. His parents, Christine Delouvin and Jacky Hénin, combined their vineyards and founded Champagne Hénin-Delouvin. Julien and his brother Guillaume both work on the family estate. Julien also has his own label, with just over one and a half hectares of vineyard in Cerseuil, in the Vallée de la Marne. He has farmed those vineyards organically since 2020.

His Champagnes are all Chardonnay from the clay soils of Cerseuil, and everything is made in barrel. The wines are slightly oxidative, but have plenty of freshness, more than you’d expect from this area. His first Champagne is called Jolie Promenade, after the walks along the Marne from his childhood. He also makes Le Champ Dame Jeanne, a 2020 Chardonnay without dosage, of which only about 1,200 bottles were made.
