The right glass

Wine is often drunk from the wrong glass, and then it does not show at its best. With the two Hedonist glasses you have the right glass for almost every wine, and you no longer need any other glasses.

Why Hedonist

In many wide wine glasses, the bowl does not narrow towards the top. The wine then gets too much oxygen and quickly falls apart, especially delicate wines such as red Burgundy or Champagne. The wide Hedonist glass clearly narrows at the top, so the wine gets space without falling apart. The glasses are hand-blown and lead-free, and we simply think they are really lovely glasses.

Which glass for which wine

A fresh wine is best in the narrower glass. There the wine comes into contact with less oxygen, so its freshness and tension are preserved and the aroma stays concentrated. This glass is ideal for fresh Champagnes and crisper white wines, such as Riesling.

A broader wine, on the other hand, needs more space and air to fully show itself. The wider glass is therefore suited to Champagnes with more volume, and perfect for Burgundy and Nebbiolo.
