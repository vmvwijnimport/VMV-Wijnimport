The right glass

Wine is often served in the wrong glass, and then it can’t show at its best. With Hedonist’s two glasses, you have the right glass for virtually any wine, and you won’t need any others.

Why Hedonist

In many wide wine glasses, the bowl doesn’t narrow towards the rim. The wine gets too much oxygen and quickly falls apart, especially delicate wines such as red Burgundy or Champagne. The wide Hedonist glass clearly tapers at the top, so the wine has room to open up without falling apart. The glasses are hand-blown and lead-free, and quite simply, we love drinking from them.

Which glass for which wine

Fresh wines are best in the narrower glass. The wine is exposed to less oxygen, so it keeps its freshness and tension, and the aromas stay focused. This glass is ideal for fresh Champagnes and crisper white wines, such as Riesling.

Fuller wines, on the other hand, need more room and air to show everything they have. The wider glass suits fuller-bodied Champagnes, and is perfect for Burgundy and Nebbiolo.
