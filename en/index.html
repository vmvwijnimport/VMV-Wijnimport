Wine is made in the vineyard

We import wine from a new generation of winemakers. They farm without pesticides or herbicides and intervene as little as possible in the cellar.

Meet our producers

Young winemakers

Our winemakers are part of a new generation. Some have taken over the family estate, others have started from scratch, and they all do things their own way.

The vineyard first

Our winemakers don’t use pesticides or herbicides, and they work with respect for nature, because good wine starts with a healthy vineyard.

Low intervention

In the cellar, our winemakers intervene as little as possible, so the vineyard can speak for itself.

Founded in November 2024

VMV Wijnimport is young, just like the winemakers we work with. We visit them in their vineyards, taste from barrel and only pick wines we fully stand behind.
