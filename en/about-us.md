## Our winemakers

We work with a new generation of winemakers, most of whom farm organically or biodynamically. Among other things, that means no herbicides or pesticides, so the vines grow in healthier, greener soil with more biodiversity. It’s better for the environment and for our health, and it also adds complexity to the wines. In the end, wine is largely made in the vineyard. Many of our producers share that mindset: if the work in the vineyard gives you excellent grapes, there’s little left to do in the cellar. That’s also why these wines express so clearly where they come from.

## How we work

We often hear about new winemakers through the ones we already work with, or through other people in the wine world. We then visit them, talk about how they work and taste from barrel. We only buy wines we love to drink ourselves. Some of our winemakers produce very little, and demand is high. We mainly offer those wines to restaurants and bars in the Netherlands, so that as many people as possible get to taste them.
