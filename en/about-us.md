## Our winemakers

We work with the new generation of winemakers. Most of them work organically or biodynamically. Among other things, this means they work without herbicides and pesticides, so the grapes grow in healthier, greener soil with more biodiversity. Besides being better for the environment and for health, it adds to the complexity of the wines. In the end, most of a wine is made in the vineyard. Many producers share that mentality: if the grapes are of a high standard thanks to the work in the vineyard, there is actually little to do in the cellar. That is also why these wines show best where they come from.

## How we work

We often hear about new winemakers through the winemakers we already work with, or through other people in the wine world. We then go and visit them, talk about how they work and taste from the barrel. We only buy wines we like to drink ourselves. Some winemakers make very little wine and the demand is high. We mainly offer those wines to restaurants and bars in the Netherlands, so that as many people as possible can taste them.
